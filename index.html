What Koko does

🎤

An inventory that keeps itself

Just say it — “Koko, we're out of dish soap” — and the pantry updates. Every container drains on screen as your family uses it.

🍽

Dinner from what you have

Ask what you can make without going to the store and get real answers, portioned to your household — with your own family recipes in the cookbook.

🛒

Shopping that thinks

Tell it your standards — all natural, no dyes, American sourced — and Koko builds the basket, priced per unit, ready to hand to your favorite retailer.

🖼

The family hub

Between meals, Koko is a photo frame, a calendar, and the place the whole household stays in sync — no more paper on the fridge.

Why we're building it

Families everywhere want the same things: to eat clean, waste less, source closer to home, and know exactly what they're feeding their children — without spending Sunday planning it all. Koko exists so a well-kept home keeps itself.
